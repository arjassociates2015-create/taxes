Common questions

Short answers. Your own case may differ.

Which documents do I need for ITR filing?

Usually PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16 or income proofs, Form 26AS and AIS, bank statements and investment proofs. We send you a checklist after the first call.

I received an income tax notice. What now?

Don’t ignore it, and note the response date. Send us a copy and we’ll tell you what it means and how to reply.

Do you work with clients in other cities?

Yes. We serve clients all over India. Most work happens online through calls and secure document sharing.

How are fees decided?

By scope and complexity, quoted in writing before we start.
